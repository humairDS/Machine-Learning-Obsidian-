### What is Computer Vision?

**Computer Vision (CV)** is a field of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** that enables computers to **see, understand, and interpret images and videos**, similar to how humans use their eyes and brain.

The goal of computer vision is to extract meaningful information from visual data and make decisions based on it.

**Examples:**

- Face unlock on smartphones
- Self-driving cars detecting roads and pedestrians
- Medical image analysis (X-rays, MRI scans)
- Security camera monitoring
- Object detection in photos and videos


## Main Topics in Computer Vision
### 1. Image Classification
The computer identifies **what is in an image**.

**Example:**  
Input: Cat image 🐱  
Output: "Cat"
Popular models:
- [[CNN]] (Convolutional Neural Networks)
- [[ResNet]]
- [[EfficientNet]]

### 2. Object Detection

The computer identifies **what objects are present and where they are located**.

**Example:**  
A street image:

- Car → Bounding Box
- Person → Bounding Box
- Bicycle → Bounding Box

Popular models:

- [[YOLO]]
- [[SSD]] 
- [[Faster R-CNN]] 


### 3. Image Segmentation

The computer labels **every pixel** in an image.

Types:

- **Semantic Segmentation:** Labels each pixel by class.
- **Instance Segmentation:** Separates individual objects.

Example:

- Road pixels
- Car pixels
- Building pixels

Popular models:

- [[U-Net]]
- [[Mask R-CNN]] 

### 4. Face Detection and Recognition

Detecting and identifying faces.

Applications:

- Face Unlock
- Attendance Systems
- Security Systems

Popular methods:

- [[OpenCV]]
- [[FaceNet]] 

### 5. Image Generation

Creating new images using AI.

Examples:

- AI art
- Text-to-image generation
- Deepfakes

Popular models:

- [[GANs]]
- [[Diffusion Models]] 

### 6. Image Enhancement

Improving image quality.

Tasks:

- Denoising
- Sharpening
- Color correction
- Super Resolution (4K enhancement)

Example:

- Enhancing old blurry photos

### 7. Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

Extracting text from images.

Example:  
Take a photo of a document and convert it to text.

Popular tools:

- [[Tesseract OCR]]
- [[EasyOCR]] 

### 8. Pose Estimation

Detecting body joints and human movements.

Applications:

- Fitness tracking
- Sports analysis
- Motion capture

Popular models:

- [[OpenPose]]
- [[MediaPipe]] 


### 9. Video Analysis

Understanding events in videos.

Tasks:

- Action recognition
- Tracking moving objects
- Surveillance systems

Example:  
Detecting suspicious activities in CCTV footage.



### 10. Medical Computer Vision

Analyzing medical images.

Applications:

- Tumor detection
- Disease diagnosis
- Organ segmentation

Examples:

- [[X-rays]]
- [[CT Scans]]
- [[MRI Images]]
